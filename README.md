## Motor-Vehicle-Collision-Analytics

### Project Overview

This project delivers a comprehensive data analytics solution designed to evaluate public safety through historical road accident data. The interactive Power BI dashboard processes core parameters including accident severity, vehicle types, environmental conditions, and geographic distribution to identify high-risk patterns. The goal is to provide actionable intelligence for municipal authorities, urban planners, and traffic safety enforcement teams to mitigate casualties.

### Tools & Technologies

Platform : Microsoft Excel

Business Intelligence: Power BI Desktop

Data Modeling: Power Query (ETL processing) & DAX (Time Intelligence and KPI creation)

Visualization Techniques: Multi-axis trend tracking, geospatial mapping, and breakdown donut charts

### Findings

**Headline Metrics**

 *Total Reported Accidents:* 196,331

 *Total CY Casualties:* 126,804 (An overall reduction of 8.14% compared to PY).

 *CY Accidents:* 94,153 (A decrease of 7.85% compared to PY).

**Casualty Breakdown by Severity**

 *Fatal Casualties:* 1,642 (A significant decrease of 37.61% compared to PY).

 *Serious Casualties:* 17,289 (A decrease of 11.29% compared to PY).

 *Slight Casualties:* 107,873 (A decrease of 6.94% compared to PY).

### Insights

**1. Monthly Trends (CY vs. PY)**

 * The monthly casualty trend for 2022 (CY) remains consistently lower than 2011 (PY) across almost all months.

 * Casualties peak significantly during the late autumn months, specifically October and November, before dropping sharply in December.

**2. Vehicle Type Involvement**

 * Cars are involved in the vast majority of casualties by a massive margin, accounting for 100,781 cases.

 * Bikes (10,438) and Vans (10,012) are the next highest categories, showing highly similar risk profiles in terms of volume.

 * Agricultural vehicles present the lowest casualty numbers at 224.

**3. Environmental & Locational Factors**

 * Urban vs. Rural: Nearly two-thirds of all casualties occur in Urban areas (66.4%), while Rural areas account for 33.6%.

 * Road Type: Single carriageways are overwhelmingly the most dangerous road type, responsible for the highest concentration of casualties (approaching 100,000).

 * Light Conditions: The majority of casualties happen during daylight hours (73.37%), with 26.63% occurring in dark conditions.

 * Geographic Distribution: The casualty map indicates heavy clustering around major metropolitan hubs in the United Kingdom, specifically high-density zones surrounding London, Birmingham,     Manchester, and Leeds.

### Recommendations

**1. Targeted Urban Infrastructure** Given that 66.4% of casualties occur in urban environments, traffic calming measures, improved pedestrian infrastructure, and stricter speed enforcement should be prioritized within city centers and major metropolitan clusters.

**2. Single Carriageway Safety Initiatives** Since single carriageways account for the bulk of the road type casualties, safety campaigns should focus on these roads. Potential interventions include installing physical barriers to prevent head-on collisions, clear lane markings, and targeted speed cameras.

**3. Seasonal Enforcement in Autumn** Due to the visible spike in casualties during October and November, local authorities should launch targeted road safety campaigns and increase highway policing during these months to address potential seasonal hazards like worsening weather or shorter daylight hours.

**4. Focus on Car Safety and Driver Behavior** With cars contributing to over 100,000 casualties, policy and enforcement should remain heavily focused on driver behavior, distracted driving, and ensuring vehicle safety compliance.
